========================================================
STOCKOFFLINE — PITCH SCRIPT (say it out loud, practice)
========================================================

OPENER (30 seconds):
"Sir, good morning. I built this small stock software for shops —
it keeps your items in the computer, scans barcodes, warns you when
stock is low, and gives a monthly report. No internet needed.
Can I show you in 5 minutes?"

--------------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIONS -> REPLY
--------------------------------------------------------
Shopkeeper: "We have no WiFi."
You:        "That's exactly why I built it — it runs without
            internet, even if the light goes off."

Shopkeeper: "Excel is free."
You:        "In Excel you type everything yourself. This scans it
            in — saves 10 minutes every day."

Shopkeeper: "I won't pay."
You:        "No problem sir, just see the demo. If you like it,
            Rs.499 setup, or Rs.299/month — no risk to you."

Shopkeeper: "We have no computer."
You:        "An old laptop or a Rs.3-4k mini-PC works.
            Tell me, I can arrange it."

--------------------------------------------------------
CLOSE (after demo):
"Shall I set it up now? Rs.499 one-time, or Rs.299 a month.
 I'll add your first 100 products and train you — 30 minutes."
