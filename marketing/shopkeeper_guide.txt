========================================================
STOCKOFFLINE — HOW TO USE (for the shop owner)
========================================================

1. OPEN THE APP
   Double-click the "StockOffline" icon on your desktop.
   (It needs no internet.)

2. ADD A PRODUCT (do this when new stock arrives)
   - Click "Add"
   - Type: Name, Price, How many in stock, Barcode (any number)
   - Click "Add"
   - Tip: you can also scan the barcode with a scanner.

3. SELL TO A CUSTOMER
   - Type the product name or scan the barcode in the search box
   - Click "Sell", enter quantity, click OK
   - Stock goes down automatically.

4. CHECK LOW STOCK (every morning)
   - Look at the list: items marked "LOW" need to be re-ordered.
   - This is your reminder — never run out.

5. MONTHLY REPORT
   - Click "Report" to see totals and category breakdown.
   - Click "Export" to save a file you can open in Excel.

6. NEED HELP?
   - WhatsApp the person who installed it. Support is free for 7 days.

You do NOT need internet, and your data stays on this computer.
