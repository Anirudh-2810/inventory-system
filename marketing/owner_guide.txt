========================================================
STOCKOFFLINE — OWNER / OPERATOR GUIDE (for you)
========================================================

GOAL: Earn your first rupee by installing StockOffline in local shops.

--------------------------------------------------------
1. PREPARE (do this at home, one time)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Build the .exe:  pip install pyinstaller
                   python marketing/build_exe.py
  -> gives marketing/dist/StockOffline.exe (no Python needed on shop PC)
- OR just use Python: ensure shop PC has Python, copy the src/ folder.
- Print: leaflet.txt, price_sheet.txt, shopkeeper_guide.txt, and a UPI QR.
- Practice the demo 3 times at home until it's 30 seconds smooth.

--------------------------------------------------------
2. THE DEMO FLOW (memorize)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Open StockOffline (double-click the .exe).
2. Click "Add" -> "Surf 1kg", price 45, stock 20, barcode 123 -> Add.
3. Scan/type 123 in search -> product shows.
4. Click "Sell", qty 5 -> stock 15.
5. Set reorder level 18 -> it shows "LOW" -> "Sir, see the alert!"
6. Click "Report" -> counts. Click "Export" -> CSV.

--------------------------------------------------------
3. AT THE SHOP (professional checklist)
--------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Greet, 30-sec demo (SHOW, don't explain).
[ ] Ask owner for ONE real product to add live (builds trust).
[ ] Show the LOW-stock alert — that's the "wow".
[ ] Take UPI payment on the spot; give a written receipt.
[ ] Leave: desktop shortcut + shopkeeper_guide.txt + "7-day free support".
[ ] Evening: WhatsApp thank-you + 1-line "how to add today's stock" tip.
[ ] Follow-up in 1 week — offer the Rs.299/month lease.

--------------------------------------------------------
4. YOUR WEEKLY RHYTHM (14 hrs/week)
--------------------------------------------------------
- 2 shop visits per day after classes/work (non-peak hours: 3-5pm).
- Track 3 numbers: visits, demos, rupees earned (notebook is fine).
- Sunday 30 min: which pitch line worked? Double down on it.

--------------------------------------------------------
5. SCALING (once you have 5 shops)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Convert upfront buyers to Rs.299/month lease = predictable income.
- Ask each happy shop for 1 referral.
- Later: deploy the WEB tier (Dockerfile + DEPLOY.md) so owners check
  stock from their phone. The multi-tenant system already separates
  each shop's data (owner_id).

--------------------------------------------------------
6. HONEST LIMITS (never overpromise)
--------------------------------------------------------
- v1 tracks stock & reports (CSV export). It does NOT print GST bills
  yet — that's a future Rs.999 add-on.
- Shop needs a PC (old laptop or Rs.3-4k mini-PC works).
- You don't need GST below Rs.20 lakh turnover.

FAQ answers are in pitch_script.txt — rehearse them.
